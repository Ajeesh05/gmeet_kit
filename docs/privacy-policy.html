Privacy Policy — Gmeet Kit

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Gmeet Kit ("the extension") enhances Google Meet with features such as auto-mute, auto-disable camera, saved/recent meeting links, and full-screen presentation mode. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

Data we collect

None. Gmeet Kit does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, browsing activity, or Google Meet content with the developer or any third party. There are no analytics, ad, or telemetry services embedded in the extension.

Data stored on your device

The extension uses the standard chrome.storage API to save data locally in your browser profile, including:

  • Your saved and recently joined Google Meet links (title and URL only)
  • Your feature settings (e.g., auto-mute, auto-disable camera, fit-screen preferences)
  • If you enable the optional caption/transcript recording feature, the live captions Google Meet displays during your meetings are saved as text on your device so you can review them later

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to the developer, to Google, or to any external server — there is no server-side component to this extension. If you use Chrome Sync, some of this data may sync across your own signed-in devices via your Google account, the same as any other Chrome extension storage — the developer has no access to it.

Meeting transcripts and recording consent

The caption/transcript recording feature captures what Google Meet's live captions display during a call and stores it locally, only in your own browser. It does not access audio or video streams directly. Recording or transcribing a conversation may be regulated by law in your location (for example, some jurisdictions require the consent of all participants before a meeting is recorded or transcribed). You are responsible for complying with applicable law and your organization's policies, and for informing other participants, before enabling this feature.

Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions, used strictly for the features described above:

  • activeTab / tabs — to detect and interact with the active Google Meet tab (e.g., open saved links, detect meeting state).
  • storage — to save your settings and links locally, as described above.
  • sidePanel — to display the extension's side panel UI inside Chrome.

The extension's content scripts and host permissions are limited to meet.google.com. It does not run on, or read data from, any other site.

Third parties

Gmeet Kit does not integrate with any third-party service, API, or SDK. It does not use Google Workspace APIs or request OAuth access to your Google account data.

Children's privacy

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
